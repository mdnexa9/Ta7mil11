FAQ

Good to know.

Is MD Work free?

Yes. The app is free to download and free to use. It is supported by ads, and none of the features above need a payment.

Do I need an account or internet?

No. There is no sign-up and no login. Your records are stored on your device, so the app works offline.

Which languages and currencies are supported?

The app and its reports are translated into 28 languages, including Arabic with full right-to-left support, and it works with EUR, USD, GBP, TND and AED.

Can I record hours instead of days?

Yes. Switch to “By hour” in Settings and enter the hours you worked; earnings follow your hourly rate.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No work record leaves your phone. The app does not need an account, and your days and expenses stay local.

Can I move my records to a new phone?

Yes. Export a backup file from the app, keep it somewhere safe, and restore it on the new phone. Without a backup, records stay on the old device only.

Your data

Delete your data.

MD Work has no account and no cloud. Your days, earnings and expenses live only on your phone, so you can erase them at any time — nothing is stored on our side.

  1. 01

    Delete one record

    Open the day or the expense in the app and delete it. Totals and the calendar update straight away.

  2. 02

    Delete everything in the app

    In MD Work, open Settings and tap Delete all data. Every day, expense and note is erased at once, and the action cannot be undone.

  3. 03

    Uninstall or clear storage

    Removing MD Work, or clearing its storage in Android settings, also deletes the records it keeps on the phone.

Deleting the app does not remove copies that may exist in a phone backup or in a report you shared or exported earlier. Delete those from Google Drive, your gallery or the app you sent them to.
